Rupa & Company Limited RUPA NSE

Consumer Cyclical · Apparel Manufacturing · India
https://rupa.co.in

Rupa & Company Limited, together with its subsidiaries, manufactures, markets, sells, and distributes hosiery products in knitted undergarments, casual wears, and thermal wears for men, women, and kids in India and internationally. The company offers innerwear, outerwear, lingerie, and fashion wear; vests, briefs, trunks, boxers, t-shirts, tank tops, joggers, bermudas, capris, loungers, and night wear for men; bras, camisoles, panties, t-shirts, tank tops, bottom wear, joggers, night wear, leggings, shorts, and co-ord sets for women; vests, briefs, t-shirts, infant wear, shorts, loungers, and trunks for kids; winter wear comprising thermals, hoodies, jackets, and sweatshirts; and accessories, including socks, towels and handkerchiefs, and rainsuits. It provides its products under the Frontline, Colors, Peek-A-Boo, Euro, Jon, Bumchums, Torrido, Thermocot, Rupa Rainsuit, Softline, Macroman Original, Macrowoman W-series, Macroman M-series, Femmora, Peek-A-Boo, and Footline brands. The company also generates power through a windmill; and offers contract manufacturing services. It sells its products through dealers, retail outlets, large format stores, multi-brand and exclusive brand outlets, and e-commerce. The company exports its products. Rupa & Company Limited was founded in 1968 and is based in Kolkata, India.
