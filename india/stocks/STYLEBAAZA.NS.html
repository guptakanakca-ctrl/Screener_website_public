Baazar Style Retail Limited STYLEBAAZA NSE

Consumer Cyclical · Apparel Retail · India
https://stylebaazar.in

Baazar Style Retail Limited engages in the retail of various apparel and non-apparel consumer products in India. It offers topwear, ethnic wear, bottomwear, and nightwear for women, and for boys and girls; shirts, t-shirts, trousers, sportswear, winter wear, and accessories for men; footwear, bags, accessories, toys, stationery, and travel products; skincare, cosmetics, hygiene and grooming essentials; and home and living products, such as kitchenware, houseware, home décor, and appliances. The company operates retail stores under the Style Baazar, Express Baazar, and Mega Baazar names. Baazar Style Retail Limited was incorporated in 2013 and is headquartered in Kolkata, India.
